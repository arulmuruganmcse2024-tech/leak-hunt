# Engineering log

My own mistakes, what found them, and what changed, in the order they happened. This is not platform friction (that is in
[FRICTION_LOG.md](FRICTION_LOG.md)); it is here so a reader can see how the checks earned their place. "Found by looking" means
a person reading a screenshot found it, not a test.

| Date | Where | What happened | What I did |
